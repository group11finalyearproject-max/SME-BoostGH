5.1 Summary of the Project
This project developed SME Boost GH, a cross-platform mobile application designed to support Ghanaian entrepreneurs and small business owners in managing important day-to-day business activities. The system was built to address practical challenges faced by small and medium-sized enterprises, especially difficulties in preparing business plans, creating marketing content, writing professional emails, managing customer records, and tracking invoices.
The application was implemented using Expo and React Native for the mobile frontend, while Pythoon (FastAPI) was used for backend services. Artificial intelligence was integrated to assist users in generating business plans, marketing content, and formal business emails. Google Firebase Authentication was used to manage user sign-in and identity verification, while local persistent storage and supporting services were used to manage customer records, invoices, reports, drafts, backup, and workflow continuity.
Overall, SME Boost GH represents a practical digital solution aimed at simplifying business operations for SMEs in Ghana through a guided, easy-to-use, and modern mobile experience.

5.2 Achievements of Objectives
The study achieved its objectives in the following ways:

1. To create an easy-to-use mobile UI that improves usability by entrepreneurs with different levels of digital literacy.
This objective was largely achieved through the design of a guided mobile interface with clear navigation, onboarding steps, readable forms, dashboard-based workflows, and clear loading, empty, and feedback states. These design choices reduce cognitive load and make the application easier to use for users with low to medium digital skills.

2. To develop a cross-platform mobile application using industry-standard frameworks Expo (React Native).
This objective was achieved. The mobile application was developed with Expo and React Native, allowing one shared codebase to support cross-platform mobile development while preserving modern development standards and maintainability.

3. To generate business plans, marketing content, and professional emails using artificial intelligence technology contextualized for Ghanaian SMEs using artificial intelligence technology, marketing content and formal emails contextualized for Ghana.
This objective was achieved through the implementation of AI-powered workflows for business plan generation, marketing content creation, professional email drafting, and AI chat assistance. These features provide practical outputs that can help Ghanaian entrepreneurs communicate more professionally and plan their businesses more effectively.

4. To effectively and professionally manage customer data using a reliable CRM system.
This objective was achieved at the application level through the CRM module, which allows users to add, view, search, and manage customer information in an organized way. The system improves accessibility to customer records and supports more professional follow-up and relationship management.

5. To develop an invoicing module that generates, tracks, and emails invoices.
This objective was achieved through the invoicing module, which supports invoice creation, status tracking, reporting, and invoice sharing through channels such as email and other share options available on the device. This helps users monitor outstanding payments and improve billing organization.

6. To ensure the use of security and privacy of data using Google Firebase authentication and encrypted communication.
This objective was substantially achieved through the use of Firebase Authentication for user sign-up, sign-in, password reset, session control, and backend token verification. The application structure supports secure authenticated access to protected AI routes. Encrypted communication is expected when the system is deployed over secure HTTPS channels in production.

7. To evaluate system usability and performance by the actual users of SME to validate system effectiveness.
This objective was partially achieved. The project includes usability-focused workflows, onboarding support, user feedback states, and lightweight analytics support that can assist evaluation. However, a broader formal usability study with more users, longer observation, and detailed performance metrics would further strengthen validation of the system's effectiveness.

5.4 Limitations of the Study
Despite the value of the project, some limitations were identified:

1. The quality of AI-generated content depends on the quality of user input, prompt context, internet availability, and model response quality; therefore, human review is still necessary.
2. Some business data management features are currently centered on local device storage and supporting export or sync helpers, which may limit full multi-device synchronization in the current form.
3. Security measures are present, but full production hardening, deployment configuration, and stricter backend restrictions would still be necessary for large-scale real-world deployment.
4. The usability and performance evaluation was not extensive enough to represent all categories of SMEs across different sectors and digital skill levels in Ghana.
5. The invoicing, reporting, and CRM features are useful for small businesses, but they may not yet cover every advanced accounting, tax, or enterprise requirement.


GOOD:
Notwithstanding the importance of the project, some limitations exist. 

 1. The quality of AI-generated content depends on the quality of user input, prompt context, internet availability, and model response quality; therefore, human review is still necessary. 

 2. Some features of the business data management system currently leverage local device storage and support for export or sync helpers, preventing full multi-device synchronization in its current form. 

 3. There are already security measures in place, but a large-scale real-world deployment will still require full production hardening, deployment configuration, and stricter backend restrictions. 

 4. The assessment of usability and performance was not done to the extent to which it could represent all category of SMEs in different sectors and digital skills in Ghana. 

 5. The features for invoicing, reporting and CRM can be useful for small businesses, but they may not currently cover every advanced accounting, tax, or enterprise need. 



5.5 Recommendation for Future Work
Future work on SME Boost GH should focus on strengthening both functionality and large-scale readiness. First, the system can be improved by introducing full cloud-based synchronization for customer records, invoices, drafts, and reports so that users can securely access their data across multiple devices. Second, the AI module can be enhanced with more localized business intelligence, stronger prompt design, and deeper Ghana-specific financial and market guidance to improve the relevance of generated outputs. Third, future versions can include more advanced CRM and accounting features such as payment reminders, expense tracking, tax estimation, receipt management, and richer business analytics dashboards.

In addition, future studies should carry out a broader usability and performance evaluation involving a larger number of SMEs from different sectors and levels of digital literacy across Ghana. Such evaluation would provide stronger empirical evidence on user satisfaction, effectiveness, efficiency, and adoption barriers. Finally, future deployment work should emphasize production-grade security, stricter access control, reliable cloud backup, and scalable backend infrastructure to support real-world commercial use.

GOOD:
The project should focus on enhancing large-scale and functional readiness of SME Boost GH. To begin with, the system can be enhanced by introducing complete cloud synchronization of customer records, invoices, drafts, and reports so users can safely access their records from a variety of devices.  The enhancement of localized business intelligence, stronger prompt design, and Ghana-specific investment and financial market advice could bolster the relevance of outputs generated by the AI module. In later versions, advanced CRM and accounting functions can be added that can handle payments reminders, expenses tracking, tax estimation, receipts management and richer business analytics dashboard. 

Subsequently, further research should undertake a more comprehensive evaluation of usability and performance within a larger number of SMEs of different sectors and levels of digital literacy in Ghana. It would strong empirical evidence on user satisfaction, effectiveness, efficiency, and adoption barriers. Deployment work in the future needs to focus on securing the application for production, enforcing stricter access control, a reliable cloud backup, and a scalable backend for use in commercial applications.
